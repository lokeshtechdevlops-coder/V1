# Pharmacy assistance — fictional demonstration policy

Document status: demonstration only; not a real hospital policy.
Suggested audience: public.

The medicine directory shows hospital inventory information. A stock entry does
not authorise dispensing or provide a patient-specific prescription. Patients can
read prescriptions recorded for their own account. Only an authorised doctor can
record a prescription for a patient under their care. The assistant must not invent
medicine doses, diagnoses or treatment instructions.
